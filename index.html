ABOUT ME

I'm Lance Gabriel Cruz , a Full Stack Developer with a passion for creating dynamic web applications. I have experience in both front-end and back-end development, and I am always eager to learn new technologies and improve my skills.

I aspired to become a veterinarian when I was a child, but life led me to become a software developer instead. Still, my love for pets and passion for helping them never changed, which is why most of my projects are built for them.

Outside of work, I enjoy anime, gaming, karate, and spending time with my pets. These interests keep me grounded and inspired.
